# Dusk and Dawn - Game Specification

Asynchronous turn-based strategy game for two players on a hexagonal grid. Players alternate control based on an in-game day/night cycle.

## Players and Turns

There are exactly two players per game:

| Player | Active Hours | Resource Production Trigger |
|--------|-------------|-----------------------------|
| Day    | 06:00-17:59 | Dawn (clock reaches 06:00)  |
| Night  | 18:00-05:59 | Dusk (clock reaches 18:00)  |

---

## Resources

There are six resource types:

| Resource | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Wood     | Gathered from trees, used for buildings and equipment |
| Stone    | Gathered from mountains, used for towers and walls |
| Iron     | Produced by upgraded houses (homestead+) next to mountains, used for equipment |
| Gold     | Produced by manors next to mountains, used for priests and Queen research |
| Food     | Produced by farms and fishing, used for peasants and steeds |
| Faith    | Generated by priests in churches, used for healing and summoning |

---

## Hex Grid

The game uses an **odd-r offset coordinate system**. Even rows are flush left, odd rows are shifted right.

### Neighbor Offsets

**Even rows** (row % 2 === 0):

| Direction | Row Offset | Column Offset |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| East      | 0         | +1            |
| West      | 0         | -1            |
| NorthEast | -1        | 0             |
| NorthWest | -1        | -1            |
| SouthEast | +1        | 0             |
| SouthWest | +1        | -1            |

**Odd rows** (row % 2 === 1):

| Direction | Row Offset | Column Offset |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| East      | 0         | +1            |
| West      | 0         | -1            |
| NorthEast | -1        | +1            |
| NorthWest | -1        | 0             |
| SouthEast | +1        | +1            |
| SouthWest | +1        | 0             |

## Landscape Types

| Type       | Walkable | Buildable | Notes |
|------------|----------|-----------|-------|
| grass      | Yes      | Yes       | Default buildable terrain |
| farm       | Yes      | No        | Created when a house is built adjacent to grass. Produces food for the house. |
| tree       | No       | No        | Impassable. Produces wood for an adjacent house. |
| sand       | Yes      | No        | Shoreline transition between grass and water |
| water      | No       | No        | Boat steed enables water travel |
| mountain   | No       | No        | Impassable. Produces stone for an adjacent house; iron from a homestead, gold from a manor. |
| unexplored | -        | -         | Fog of war placeholder for non-visible tiles |

Terrain is never cleared or harvested by pieces; resources come from houses working the land around them.

## Pieces

There are four piece types:

| Type        | Hearts | Attack | Defense | View | Move | Attack Range | Can Equip | Can Mount Steed | Cost |
|-------------|--------|--------|---------|------|------|-------------|-----------|-----------------|------|
| Peasant     | 1      | 1      | 0       | 1    | 1    | 1           | Yes       | Yes             | 1 food (spawn from house) |
| King        | 3      | 1      | 1       | 2    | 1    | 1           | No        | Yes             | Starts in game |
| Priest      | 3      | 0      | 0       | 1    | 1    | 1           | No        | Yes             | 1 gold (train from church) |
| Arch Angel  | 3      | 3      | 3       | 3    | 3    | 1           | No        | No              | 100 faith + 10 praying priests |

### Special Abilities

- **King**: Can enter a tower to create a castle.
- **Priest**: Can heal adjacent allies (costs 1 faith per heal). Generates 1 faith when praying in a church.
- **Arch Angel**: Summoned in an empty friendly church when requirements are met.

---

## Buildings

There are six building types. All buildings have 1 defense and 0 hearts.

| Type   | Cost                | View Range | Walkable By  | Special |
|--------|---------------------|------------|-------------|---------|
| House  | 1 wood              | 1          | Both players | Spawns peasants. Adjacent grass converts to farm. |
| Tower  | 10 stone            | 4          | Both players | King enters to create castle. Bow in tower gets range 4. |
| Castle | Free (from tower)   | 3          | Both players | Research hub. Castle destroyed = king inside dies. |
| Wall   | 1 stone             | 0          | Owner only   | Defensive barrier. |
| Church | 3 wood + 3 stone    | 1          | Both players | Trains priests. Priests inside generate faith. |
| Dock   | 4 wood              | 1          | Both players | Built on sand next to water. Builds boats. |

---

## Equipment

Equipment is for peasants only. Each peasant can carry at most one of each type.

| Type   | Cost           | Effect |
|--------|---------------|--------|
| Sword  | 1 iron         | +1 attack |
| Shield | 1 wood         | +1 defense |
| Bow    | 1 wood + 1 iron | +1 attack range. In a tower: range becomes tower view range (4). |

---

## Steeds

Horses are bought at houses and placed on an adjacent tile; boats are built at docks and placed on adjacent water. Pieces pick them up by moving onto the tile.

| Type  | Cost    | Effect |
|-------|---------|--------|
| Horse | 10 food | +1 view, +1 move, +1 bow range |
| Boat  | 10 wood | +1 view, +1 move, +1 bow range. Enables water travel. |

---

## Research

Research requires a castle. There are four research options.

| Research   | Cost    | Prerequisite | Effect |
|------------|---------|-------------|--------|
| Speed      | 1 wood  | None        | Reduces action time. 6 levels: 60/30/15/10/5/1 min per action. |
| Queen      | 25 gold | None        | All tiles adjacent to buildings become visible. |

---

## Clock System

The game uses a 24-hour clock starting at 06:00. Each action advances the clock by a number of minutes determined by the current speed research level.

| Phase | Hours | Active Player |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| Day   | 06-17 | Day player   |
| Night | 18-05 | Night player |

- **Dawn (06:00)**: Day player receives resource production. Control passes to day player.
- **Dusk (18:00)**: Night player receives resource production. Control passes to night player.

### Speed Levels

| Level | Minutes per Action |
|-------|-------------------|
| 0     | 60                |
| 1     | 30                |
| 2     | 15                |
| 3     | 10                |
| 4     | 5                 |
| 5     | 1                 |

---

## Resource Production

Resource production triggers at dawn (for day player) and dusk (for night player).

### Production Rules

- **House next to a farm**: +1 food per adjacent farm tile
- **House next to a forest**: +1 wood per adjacent tree tile
- **House next to a mountain**: +1 stone per adjacent mountain tile
- **Homestead** (house upgraded for 3 wood + 2 stone): +2 stone and +1 iron per adjacent mountain
- **Manor** (homestead upgraded for 5 wood + 5 stone + 1 iron): additionally +1 gold per mountain, and +2 wood / +2 food per forest / farm
- Each terrain tile produces once per cycle even if several houses touch it
- **Priest in church**: +1 faith
- **Peasant in boat on water**: +1 food

## Combat

### Damage Resolution

1. Damage is applied to defense first, then hearts.
2. Defense loss is permanent (does not regenerate).
3. A piece with 0 hearts is destroyed.

### Building Combat

- Buildings have 1 defense and 0 hearts.
- Requires attack greater than 1 to destroy a building.
- When a castle is destroyed, the king inside dies.

### Target Priority

When attacking a tile:
1. Enemy building on the tile is targeted first.
2. Then the enemy piece on the tile.

### Attack Range

- Base attack range comes from the piece type.
- Equipment modifies range (bow adds +1).
- A piece with a bow inside a tower uses the tower view range (4) as attack range.

## Actions

There are thirteen actions available:

| Action            | Description | Cost |
|-------------------|------------|------|
| Move              | Move piece to adjacent walkable tile. Can mount steeds on arrival. | - |
| Build             | Place building on grass inside your kingdom (a tile you can see). | Varies by building |
| SpawnPeasant      | Create peasant in an empty friendly house, if there is housing for it (1 slot per house, 2 per homestead, 3 per manor). | 1 food |
| CraftEquipment    | Equip a peasant with sword, shield, or bow. | Varies by equipment |
| BuySteed          | Place a horse next to a friendly house, or a boat next to a friendly dock. | Varies by steed |
| TrainPriest       | Create priest in an empty friendly church. | 1 gold |
| Heal              | Priest heals adjacent ally by 1 heart. | 1 faith |
| Research          | Unlock research at castle if affordable and prerequisites met. | Varies by research |
| EnterTower        | King enters adjacent tower, creating a castle. | - |
| SummonArchAngel   | Summon in an empty friendly church. | 100 faith + 10 praying priests |
| Attack            | Damage enemy piece or building within attack range. | - |
| UpgradeBuilding   | Raise one of your houses to the next level (homestead, manor). | 3 wood + 2 stone, then 5 wood + 5 stone + 1 iron |
| Pass              | Let one hour pass, or (`toPhaseEnd`) end your whole phase. Always legal on your turn, so a phase can never get stuck. | - |

## Win Conditions

A player loses if either condition is met:

- Their king is dead.
- They have no pieces AND no houses.

---

## Fog of War

Tiles are visible within the view range of a player's own pieces and buildings. Non-visible tiles are shown as unexplored.

### Queen Research

When Queen research is unlocked, all tiles adjacent to the player's buildings become visible.

---

## Initial Setup

### Starting Resources

Each player begins with: **5 wood, 2 stone**.

### Starting Conditions

- Clock starts at 06:00. Day player goes first.
- **Day player**: King + peasant placed near bottom-left of the map.
- **Night player**: King + peasant placed near top-right of the map.
- Trees and mountains are cleared from the starting area around each king.
- Pieces may walk onto and through their own buildings; enemy walls block.

---

## Map Generation

Maps are procedurally generated using noise-based generation with three noise layers (elevation, forest, mountain) combined with a hexagonal island mask. Forests and mountains form small clusters, and after generation every patch of walkable land is joined to the rest by carving grass corridors through the nearest trees or mountains, so there is always a pass — only water separates land.

Configurable parameters:
- Forest density
- Mountain density
- Water level

See [MAP_ENGINE.md](MAP_ENGINE.md) for full details.
